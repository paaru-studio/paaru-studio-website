CAMPAIGN SPOTLIGHT

Sugarfit New Protein Powder Launch — Influencer Campaign

📦 Product Launch 📱 Instagram Reels 🏋️ Health & Wellness

Paaru Studio-Media partnered with a leading influencer marketing agency to power the launch of Sugarfit's new protein powder — an innovative health supplement designed for fitness-conscious consumers seeking a cleaner, smarter nutrition solution. For this campaign, we curated a select network of fitness and lifestyle creators whose authentic content style perfectly aligned with Sugarfit's brand values of transparency, performance, and wellbeing. Each creator received a tailored campaign brief, ensuring their storytelling felt natural and persuasive rather than promotional. The resulting short-form content drove exceptional reach across Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, sparking conversations among health communities and translating into measurable product awareness and direct conversions. This collaboration showcases how Paaru Studio-Media's end-to-end influencer management — from creator matching and brief development to content review and performance reporting — turns a product launch into a viral brand moment.
