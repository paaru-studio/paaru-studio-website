Corporate Video Production FAQ

How much does a corporate film or company profile video cost in Bangalore? +
Corporate video production packages in Bangalore typically range from ₹1,20,000 to ₹3,50,000 for single-day executive interviews, leadership profiles, or customer case studies. Full-scale corporate brand documentaries and multi-facility tech park shoots across India typically range from ₹4,00,000 to ₹12,00,000+ depending on scriptwriting, animation VFX, drone filming permits, and linguistic localization.
How do you handle filming without disrupting our daily office operations? +
We plan call sheets down to 15-minute increments. Our cinematographers utilize wireless battery-powered cinema rigs and lightweight LED softboxes that set up in under 20 minutes, allowing us to film boardrooms, breakout lounges, and executive suites during pre-scheduled windows without causing distraction to your staff.
Can you film across multiple branch offices and plants outside Bangalore? +
Yes. Paaru Studio operates a multi-city commercial production network across India, including Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, and Pune. We ensure consistent art direction, lens packages, and lighting style guides across all locations.
Do you provide executive coaching for leadership who are uncomfortable on camera? +
Yes. Our directors are trained in conversational interviewing techniques. We utilize professional teleprompter systems with custom speed controls, acoustic monitoring, and guided conversation prompts that allow your leaders to speak naturally and authoritatively without memorizing rigid scripts.
