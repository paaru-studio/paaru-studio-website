FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. Video production typically takes 2-4 weeks, while e-commerce photography can be completed in 1-2 weeks. We'll provide a detailed timeline in our proposal.

What is your payment structure?

We typically work with a 50% advance payment and 50% upon project completion. For larger projects, we can discuss milestone-based payments.

Do you provide revisions?

Yes, we include a specified number of revisions in our proposals to ensure you're completely satisfied with the final deliverables.

Can you work with our existing brand guidelines?

Absolutely! We can work with your existing brand guidelines and assets to ensure consistency across all deliverables.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, we're happy to sign Non-Disclosure Agreements to protect your confidential information and intellectual property.

What if I need rush delivery?

We can accommodate rush projects based on our current workload. Rush delivery may involve additional fees, which we'll discuss upfront.
