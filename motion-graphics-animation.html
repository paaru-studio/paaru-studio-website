Common Inquiries

Motion Graphics & Animation FAQ

How much does a 60-second animated explainer video cost in Bangalore? +
A custom 60-second 2D motion graphics explainer video in Bangalore typically ranges from ₹80,000 to ₹2,50,000 depending on character animation complexity, custom illustration style, and script length. Advanced 3D product CGI animations and complex FinTech isometric reels range between ₹2,00,000 and ₹6,00,000+. We provide itemized pricing proposals with transparent revision milestones.
What is the standard production timeline for an animation project? +
A typical 60-second explainer video takes 2 to 3 weeks from kickoff to final render. The timeline breaks down into: Script & Concept (3 days), Styleframes & Storyboarding (4 days), Voiceover Recording & Rough Animation (5 days), and Sound Design & Final 4K Render (3 days). For rush product launches, we also offer 7-day expedited delivery.
What software and render engines do your animators use? +
Our 3D pipeline utilizes Cinema 4D, Blender 4.0, and Maya powered by GPU renderers like Redshift and Octane Render for photorealistic global illumination. For 2D animation, kinetic typography, and VFX compositing, we leverage Adobe After Effects, Illustrator, and DaVinci Resolve Studio.
Do we need to supply a finished script, or do you write it? +
Most clients come to us with just a product brief, slide deck, or website link. Our senior screenwriters interview your product managers and translate your value proposition into an engaging, narrative script with exact on-screen visual directions.
