Our Story & Purpose

Crafting Stories That Scale Brands

Paaru Studio-Media is a Bangalore-based creative advertising and video production agency. Founded in 2015, we bridge cinematic filmmaking craft with quantifiable commercial results.

A Decade of Creative Excellence

Founded in 2015 in Bangalore, Paaru Studio-Media began with a single high-definition camera, a dedicated post-production workstation, and an uncompromising conviction: commercial advertising shouldn't compromise on artistic storytelling, and cinematic beauty should deliver undeniable business returns.

Over 11+ years of steady growth, we have expanded from an agile boutique production crew into a full-funnel media powerhouse. Today, our team handles television commercials (TVCs), OTT campaign films, product launch films, social reels, performance ad funnels, and one of South India's premier out-of-home (OOH and DOOH) advertising networks.

Having delivered more than 300+ campaigns for enterprise leaders and hyper-growth disruptors alike—including Japfa, Calvin Klein, GIVA, Onsurity, and Herbalife—we continue to push the boundaries of what a modern creative agency can achieve.

Meet the Founder

Sadanand B Sakkarshetty

Proprietor & Creative Director, Paaru Studio-Media

Sadanand B Sakkarshetty founded Paaru Studio-Media with a clear vision: to establish Bangalore’s most versatile, dependable, and forward-thinking visual advertising studio. Drawing on an extensive background in cinematography, commercial visual arts, and media production, Sadanand recognized an acute gap in the market. Established agencies were slow, bureaucratic, and detached from execution, while freelance crews lacked the strategic rigor and distribution intelligence required to move modern business metrics.

Starting the studio in Bangalore in 2015, Sadanand championed an integrated approach where writing, set design, camera craft, sound engineering, and visual effects happen under one collaborative roof. This direct, maker-first ethos enabled Paaru Studio-Media to deliver broadcast-caliber commercial films with unprecedented speed and cost-efficiency.

Today, Sadanand actively leads the agency’s creative vision and executive client partnerships. He personally directs tentpole commercial projects while pioneering the studio's early adoption of AI-augmented filmmaking—combining cutting-edge neural previsualization, virtual environments, and multi-lingual voice synthesis with timeless cinematic craftsmanship.
