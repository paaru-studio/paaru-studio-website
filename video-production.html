Frequently Asked Questions

How much does corporate video production or ad film making cost in Bangalore?

+
Commercial video production pricing in Bangalore typically ranges from ₹1,00,000 for focused digital promo videos and executive interviews to ₹3,50,000 – ₹15,00,000+ for large-scale television commercials, multi-location brand films, and high-VFX 3D product launches. Total costs depend directly on crew size, cinema camera configurations, location permits across Bengaluru, talent casting, set design, and the level of post-production required. We provide transparent, itemized production budgets within 24 hours of your initial creative briefing.

How long does the video production process typically take from script to delivery?

+
Turnaround timelines depend on production complexity. A corporate profile, customer testimonial, or social media campaign typically requires 10 to 18 business days from kickoff through final delivery. Larger commercial TVCs and multi-location brand films normally run 3 to 5 weeks to accommodate script iterations, casting calls, location scouting across Karnataka, and detailed VFX mastering. For time-sensitive product launches and press announcements, we also offer an expedited production track with dedicated daily edits.

What sets Paaru Studio-Media apart from other video production companies in Bangalore?

+
Unlike conventional agencies that outsource technical execution or single-discipline production shops with limited strategic foresight, Paaru Studio-Media pairs in-house creative directors and screenwriters with our own cinema-grade camera packages, lighting gear, and editing suites. This unified model guarantees visual coherence, faster revision cycles, broadcast-grade QC, and competitive pricing without middleman markups. Our 11+ years of commercial production experience in Bangalore spans Fortune 500 enterprises, tech unicorns, and direct-to-consumer disruptors.

Yes, our production team oversees every milestone from raw creative spark to final master delivery. If you begin with just an open concept or business goal, our screenwriters craft compelling shooting scripts, visual moodboards, and scene-by-scene storyboards. Do you assist with broadcast clearances and OTT technical compliance for TV commercials?

+
Yes. When producing television commercials (TVCs) and OTT advertisements for networks like Disney+ Hotstar, SonyLIV, JioCinema, and satellite broadcasters, we ensure full compliance with ASCI advertising codes and technical broadcast delivery standards (including EBU R128 loudness standards, stereo/5.1 surround mixes, and ProRes 422 HQ / 4444 delivery specifications).

What is the difference between TVC, DVC, and UGC videos, and how do we choose?

+
TVCs (Television Commercials) are high-concept 30–60 second narrative ad films built for national network broadcast with strict ASCI clearances. DVCs (Digital Video Commercials) are cinematic, high-bitrate spots engineered for OTT platforms (Hotstar, JioCinema) and unskippable YouTube placements. UGC (User-Generated Content) videos are raw, authentic 9:16 vertical smartphone reels created with relatable talent designed to maximize ROAS and lower CAC on paid social channels (Meta, TikTok). For full breakdowns and comparison metrics, visit our TVC, DVC & UGC Hub.
