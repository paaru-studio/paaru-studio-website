Frequently Asked Questions

How much does video production cost in Bangalore? +
Video production costs in Bangalore vary based on scope. A short digital ad film starts from ₹1 lakh, while a full TV commercial can range from ₹3 lakh to ₹15 lakh+. Contact us for a free estimate within 24 hours.
How long does it take to produce a corporate video? +
A typical corporate video takes 7–14 days from concept to delivery. TV commercials may take 3–4 weeks including pre-production. We also offer expedited timelines for urgent campaigns.
What types of videos does Paaru Studio produce? +
We produce TV commercials, digital ad films, corporate films, product videos, motion graphics, explainer videos, video podcasts, event coverage, and social media content for brands across Bangalore and India.
Do you handle scriptwriting and concept development? +
Yes. We handle the complete process — from discovery and scriptwriting to storyboarding, shoot, post-production, VFX, and final delivery. You don't need to come with a ready brief.
