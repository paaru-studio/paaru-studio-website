Scaling Auram Jewellery with Meta Ads & High-Intent SEO

How data-led performance funnels, dynamic Meta ad creatives, and technical e-commerce SEO unlocked a 4.2x ROAS for high-ticket fine jewellery.

Meta Ads (IG & FB) Performance Marketing E-Commerce SEO
Client: Auram Jewellery
Industry: Luxury D2C Fine Jewellery & Diamonds
Core Channel: Meta Advantage+ & Organic Search

The Challenge in Luxury Digital Acquisition

Fine jewellery is inherently high-trust, high-consideration. Prospective buyers rarely purchase gold and diamond pieces on an impulse click. Auram Jewellery had exceptional craftsmanship and bridal designs, but faced rising Meta CPMs and fierce competition from legacy retail chains.

Paaru Studio-Media was brought on board to architect a dual-engine acquisition flywheel: high-converting Meta Ad creative funnels paired with deep technical e-commerce SEO to dominate organic search when buyers were actively hunting for certified diamond jewellery.

The Two-Pronged Growth Strategy

1. Full-Funnel Meta Ads Engine (Instagram & Facebook)

We designed a tiered creative pipeline: top-of-funnel cinematic macro reels showcasing jewelry sparkle and craftsmanship; middle-of-funnel customer unboxing & hallmarking certifications to dismantle skepticism; and bottom-of-funnel dynamic catalog ads retargeting abandoned carts with limited-run offers.

  • Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns segmented by price-tier cohorts
  • Lookalike audiences built from highest-LTV repeat buyers
  • Weekly creative refreshes testing hook variations to combat ad fatigue

2. High-Intent Luxury Jewellery SEO Architecture

We executed a comprehensive technical and on-page SEO overhaul targeting high-intent commercial keywords such as "custom diamond engagement rings Bangalore" and "certified solitaire pendants online".

  • Core Web Vitals acceleration (speed scores improved from 48 to 94 on mobile)
  • Product Schema & Review Markup implementation for rich Google search snippets
  • Editorial content cluster strategy establishing brand domain authority in fine jewelry
