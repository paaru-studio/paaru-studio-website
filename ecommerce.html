Frequently Asked Questions

How much does ecommerce product photography cost in Bangalore?

+
Ecommerce product photography costs in Bangalore depend on the number of SKUs, product type, and shoot complexity. Basic white background catalog shoots start from ₹500 per product. Lifestyle and A+ content packages are available for larger catalogs. Contact Paaru Studio-Media for a customised quote within 24 hours.

Do you provide Amazon A+ content creation services?

+
Yes. Paaru Studio-Media creates Amazon A+ content including comparison modules, narrative storytelling layouts, lifestyle imagery, and infographic design — all optimized to increase conversion rates on Amazon India.

Which ecommerce platforms do you create content for?

+
We create ecommerce content for Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Shopify, Meesho, and other major platforms. All content is optimized to meet each platform's specific image and video guidelines.

What is included in an ecommerce catalog shoot?

+
Our ecommerce catalog shoots include white background product photography, multi-angle shots, lifestyle context images, infographic design, A+ content layouts, and optionally unboxing or product demo videos — all delivered in platform-ready formats.

How quickly can you deliver ecommerce product photos?

+
Standard turnaround for ecommerce product photography is 3–5 working days after the shoot. Rush delivery is available for urgent catalog launches. Contact us to discuss your timeline.
