What is the difference between OOH and DOOH advertising?

+
OOH (Out-of-Home) refers to traditional outdoor advertising like hoardings, transit ads, and building wraps. DOOH (Digital Out-of-Home) refers to digital screens in malls, gated societies, IT parks, and other locations that display dynamic digital content. Paaru Studio-Media offers both OOH and DOOH solutions across Bangalore and South India.
