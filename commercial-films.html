Answers to Common Questions

Commercial & UGC Video Production FAQ

How much does it cost to produce a TV commercial vs. a digital ad (DVC) in Bangalore? +
TV commercial production in Bangalore typically ranges from ₹4,50,000 to ₹25,00,000+ depending on talent casting, celebrity endorsements, location sets across Karnataka, and VFX scale. Digital video commercials (DVCs) tailored for OTT and YouTube generally range from ₹1,50,000 to ₹6,00,000, while UGC creator batches start at ₹45,000 for multi-hook social ad packages.
Do you handle ASCI clearance and satellite television broadcast delivery? +
Yes. We handle end-to-end ASCI clearance, legal claim substantiation, EBU R128 audio loudness certification (-24 LKFS), and format mastering across ProRes 422 HQ and 4444 for national Indian broadcasters including Star, Sony, Sun Network, and Zee.
How does your UGC video creation service work for direct-to-consumer brands? +
Our direct-response team analyzes your top-converting customer pain points, writes 3 to 5 tailored script variations per product, pairs you with vetted on-camera creators from our network, and directs filming with native 9:16 mobile capture. We deliver complete ad sets with burned-in subtitles, captions, and 3 different opening hooks for immediate Meta and TikTok A/B testing.
Can you adapt a TV commercial into vertical digital cutdowns for social feeds? +
Absolutely. During pre-production, our cinematographers frame shots using dual safe zones (16:9 widescreen and 9:16 vertical). In post-production, we deliver full-length widescreen masters alongside native 9:16 vertical cuts, 1:1 square edits, and 6-second YouTube bumper cuts.
