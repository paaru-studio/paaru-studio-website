Onsurity | Motion Graphics Showcase

Client: Onsurity Healthcare
Industry: HealthTech & Employee Benefits
Format: 2D Motion Graphics Explainer

The Project Objective

Onsurity is redefining employee healthcare benefits for startups and SMEs. They needed a clear, engaging way to explain their digital platform and the benefits of their membership, turning to our team for comprehensive corporate video production in Bangalore.

The Solution & Creative Execution

Paaru Studio-Media created a vibrant motion graphics explainer video that breaks down complex insurance concepts into simple, friendly visuals. Our design team developed custom illustrated character assets, intuitive UI representations, and rhythmic kinetic typography, accompanied by professional voiceover and tailored sound design to maintain high viewer retention.

Deliverables

  • 2D motion graphics explainer video
  • Digital website & onboarding video asset
  • Custom character animations and vector UI elements
