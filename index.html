Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Paaru Studio offer? +
We offer end-to-end Video Production (Commercials, Corporate Films, Social Media Content), E-commerce Solutions (Product Shoots, Cataloguing), and Media Buying (OOH/DOOH) services tailored for premium brands.
Does Paaru Studio handle end-to-end production? +
Yes, we handle everything from research, scriptwriting, and storyboarding to professional filming, post-production, VFX, and final delivery. We ensure a seamless experience with zero ambiguity.
What is the typical turnaround time for a project? +
Turnaround time depends on the scope, but digital ad films typically take 7-14 days from concept to final delivery. We also offer expedited schedules for specific high-priority campaigns.
Do you provide E-commerce cataloguing services? +
Absolutely. We specialize in high-end product cinematography and automated catalogue solutions for platforms like Amazon, Shopify, and Myntra, ensuring your products stand out with visual excellence.
Where are you located and do you work globally? +
Our head office is in Bangalore, India, but we serve clients nationwide and globally, delivering world-class visual content for diverse markets across different continents.
How can I get a quote for my project? +
You can fill out our 'Get Started' form on the website or reach out directly at info@paarustudio.com. We typically respond with a preliminary estimate and consultation within 24 hours.
