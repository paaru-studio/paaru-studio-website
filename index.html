Frequently Asked Questions

What does a creative and advertising agency do?

+
A creative and advertising agency develops strategic brand positioning, produces compelling visual campaigns, and executes multi-channel marketing to drive business growth. This includes scripting and producing ad films, managing digital performance ads, coordinating influencer collaborations, designing creative brand assets, and deploying outdoor billboards and digital transit media across target markets.

How do I choose an advertising agency in Bengaluru?

+
Choose an advertising agency based on demonstrated portfolio depth, cross-channel execution capabilities, local market familiarity, and transparent project communication. Evaluate their track record across both commercial video production and multi-platform media buying. The ideal partner should align with your brand objectives, respect your production timelines, and deliver measurable performance outcomes.

How much does an ad film cost in Bengaluru?

+
Ad film production costs in Bengaluru depend on project scope, concept complexity, cast requirements, crew size, location scouting, equipment tier, and post-production demands like animation or VFX. Rather than fixed rates, budgets reflect custom creative requirements for each brand. We assess your specific objectives and deliver a transparent, itemized commercial quote.

How long does an ad film take?

+
An ad film typically takes 7 to 14 days from initial creative concept approval to final edited master delivery. The exact timeframe varies depending on pre-production planning, filming schedule, cast availability, and advanced post-production requirements such as color grading and sound design. Expedited delivery schedules are available for time-sensitive brand launches.

What is OOH and DOOH advertising, and where can I advertise in Bengaluru?

+
Out-of-Home (OOH) covers static physical billboards, while Digital Out-of-Home (DOOH) uses dynamic digital screens in high-traffic urban areas. In Bengaluru, we deploy outdoor and transit placements across prime arterial routes, tech parks, co-working spaces, and premium residential hubs including Indiranagar, Koramangala, Whitefield, Outer Ring Road, MG Road, and Electronic City.

Do you handle Bangalore Metro and mall advertising?

+
Yes, we manage both Namma Metro transit advertising and prime shopping mall media installations across Bengaluru. Metro branding includes train wraps, in-train displays, and high-visibility station static and digital signages. Mall advertising encompasses digital kiosks, atrium banners, food court screens, and experiential on-ground activations in major commercial destinations citywide.

Do you run influencer marketing campaigns?

+
Yes, we run end-to-end influencer marketing campaigns tailored to your target audience and industry vertical. Our team manages creator discovery, contract negotiations, creative briefing, content production supervision, campaign rollout, and performance analytics. We partner with nano, micro, macro, and celebrity creators across Bengaluru and pan-India to drive authentic engagement and conversions.

Do you do e-commerce content for Amazon, Flipkart and Shopify?

+
Yes, we produce conversion-driven e-commerce visual assets optimized specifically for Amazon, Flipkart, Shopify, and Myntra. Our studio delivers professional tabletop and lifestyle product photography, Amazon A+ enhanced brand content, infographics, and short-form demonstration videos compliant with platform-specific listing guidelines to elevate search rankings and sales conversions.

Do you work with startups as well as large brands?

+
Yes, we collaborate with early-stage venture-backed startups, mid-market challengers, and large legacy enterprises alike. Whether you are bootstrapping an initial product launch video, executing a citywide outdoor blitz, or scaling digital customer acquisition campaigns, we adapt our creative workflows and production scale to match your business stage and growth objectives.

Where is your office, and do you work outside Bengaluru?

+
Our primary studio is located at 48B, 100 Feet Road, Nagarabhavi, Bengaluru 560072, Karnataka. While our creative headquarters is rooted in Bengaluru, we regularly handle commercial video shoots, influencer campaigns, and media deployment projects for clients across India as well as overseas international brand partners.

Does Paaru Studio-Media handle end-to-end production?

+
Yes, we manage complete end-to-end production in-house from preliminary concept development and scriptwriting to post-production delivery. Our scope covers storyboarding, casting, location permits, cinematography, sound design, visual effects, and format adaptations for cinema, television, social media, and digital out-of-home platforms without requiring third-party handoffs.
