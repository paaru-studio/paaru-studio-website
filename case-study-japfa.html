How We Helped Japfa Best Increase Brand Awareness and Boost Sales

Insights into Japfa's Success Story

Client

Japfa Best

Industry

Food & Beverage

Services

Performance Marketing, Social Media Marketing

Duration

1 Month Campaign

The Challenge

When we first connected with Japfa Best, they were facing a major hurdle. Despite offering premium chicken products, their brand remained largely unnoticed in a highly competitive market.

Without a strong marketing strategy, their message wasn't reaching potential customers, resulting in stagnant sales and limited growth opportunities.

Japfa Best needed a way to amplify brand visibility and drive measurable sales results through a clear, impactful marketing approach.

Understanding the Challenge

After analyzing Japfa Best's situation, it became clear that the core issue wasn't product quality—it was brand visibility.

In a competitive market, their products were often overshadowed by competitors with stronger online presence and more targeted campaigns.

The digital marketing landscape had evolved; customers now seek brands that connect emotionally and tell meaningful stories. Japfa Best's marketing efforts weren't effectively reaching or engaging their target audience.

They needed a clear, strategic marketing approach that would:

  • Highlight their product strengths
  • Strengthen their online presence
  • Build a connection with their audience through storytelling

That's where we stepped in to help Japfa Best redefine their marketing strategy and stand out in the digital space.

Our Strategic Approach

Our goal was clear: Build brand awareness and drive measurable sales growth.

We leveraged digital platforms to reach the right audience with the right message, combining visual storytelling, targeted advertising, and data-driven execution for maximum impact.

Meta Advertising

Focused on building brand awareness and driving engagement through targeted social media campaigns on Facebook and Instagram platforms.

Google Ads Campaign

Implemented traffic campaigns to drive website visits and increase conversion opportunities with precision targeting.

YouTube Marketing

Created engaging video content to reach audiences and build brand recall through consistent messaging and visual storytelling.

Programmatic Advertising

Used appographic programmatic targeting to reach users of apps like Licious, Green Chick Chop, and KFC, ensuring high-intent audience engagement.

The Results & Impact

Our comprehensive digital marketing strategy delivered exceptional results across all platforms, exceeding Japfa Best's initial expectations and setting new benchmarks for their brand performance.

The Meta campaigns achieved an impressive reach of 30-40% of the target audience with an ad frequency of 3, meaning the same audience saw the ads multiple times, reinforcing brand recall and recognition.

This drove consistent engagement and strengthened brand awareness across social platforms, delivering a 2x ROAS in just one month.

The Google Ads traffic campaign significantly boosted sales and attracted new user demographics with a CTR of 0.8%+ and click to traffic percentage of 50%.

Through our YouTube Campaign, we helped Japfa Best expand its customer base and increase brand loyalty with a frequency of 3-4 in a month and a reach of 60%.

This created a strong foundation for sustained engagement and growth in future campaigns.

Programmatic Targeting Excellence

We used appographic programmatic targeting to reach users of apps like Licious, Green Chick Chop, and KFC, or visitors of similar sites.

This approach ensured the campaign reached high-intent audiences already interested in chicken products.

Result: Higher relevance, stronger engagement, and better conversion potential

"Paaru Studio transformed our digital presence and helped us achieve results we never thought possible in such a short time. The strategic approach and execution were exceptional."

Key Takeaways

  • Strategic targeting is crucial - reaching high-intent audiences through programmatic advertising significantly improved conversion rates
  • Multi-channel approach delivers better results - combining Meta, Google, YouTube, and programmatic ads created comprehensive brand coverage
  • Frequency matters - consistent exposure (3-4x monthly) reinforced brand recall and drove better engagement
  • Data-driven decisions - continuous optimization based on real-time metrics delivered exceptional 2x ROAS
  • Brand storytelling - connecting emotionally with audiences through compelling content drove meaningful engagement
